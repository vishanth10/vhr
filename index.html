01 · About

Making LLMs safe and fast.

I'm a machine learning engineer working where model research meets production systems. At Zscaler I build and fine-tune the AI guardrail models in AI Guard, which stop jailbreak and prompt-injection attacks before they reach an LLM. I also make those models fast and cheap at scale: my GPU inference optimisations doubled throughput, halved latency and save roughly $2M a year.

Before that I built and benchmarked inference engines for frontier open models (Llama 4, DeepSeek V3/R1) on DGX H100 at Cornami. I ran causal-inference research on payments at Disney, built RAG products at traversaal.ai, and spent two years building AIOps systems at Citi.

I hold an MS in Business Analytics (Data Science) from UCLA Anderson and a B.Tech from NIT Trichy, so I care as much about the business impact as the model metrics.

🛡️

AI Guardrails & Security

I build and fine-tune AI guardrail models that detect jailbreaks, prompt injection and data leakage in Zscaler's AI Guard.

⚡

Inference Optimisation

GPU optimisation with TensorRT, ONNX and vLLM: 2× RPS, ~50% lower latency and ~$2M/year saved.

Distributed Serving

Tensor & data parallelism, FSDP, KV caching, flash attention and quantization for 70B+ models.

MLOps

Taking models from research notebooks to reliable, monitored production systems.
